## What this service is

A free, best-effort, single-maintainer hobby deployment of an open-source project. Not a company,
not a product with a support contract, and not something to build anything load-bearing on top of.
Saying so up front is more useful than a disclaimer nobody reads at the bottom.

## No warranty, no uptime promise

The service is provided **as is**, with no warranty of any kind, and no guarantee of availability,
durability or correctness. The free tier spins down when idle, has no persistent disk, and can be
withdrawn or reset without notice.

Concretely, and measured rather than hedged: uploaded files do not survive a restart, so a project
whose embedding settings you later change may be unable to rebuild its index. `DEPLOY.md` explains
the mechanism. Keep your own copies of anything you upload.

## Your account and your keys

- One account is one person. Do not share credentials.
- **You are responsible for the provider API keys you store**, and for what is spent on them. If
  you store a key, your traffic runs under your own agreement with that provider and their terms
  govern it.
- Keys you mint (`sk-sent-…`) are bearer credentials. Anyone holding one can use your projects and
  spend on your provider key. Revoke a key you have lost.
- **The game route carries the key in the URL path**, so it can land in proxy and access logs.
  That is a deliberate, documented trade-off for Mantella compatibility — see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — and it is a reason to treat these keys as low-trust and rotate them.

- Use the service to generate content targeting a real person, to produce sexual content involving
  minors, or for anything the underlying AI providers prohibit — their usage policies apply to your

- Resell or proxy the endpoint to third parties, or point automated load at it beyond ordinary play.
- Attempt to reach another user's projects, documents, threads or credentials. Isolation is
  enforced and audited (`tests/test_tenant_isolation.py`), and probing it is not research unless you
  are probing your own account.

Accounts doing any of the above may be removed without notice.

Model output is generated by third-party AI providers and is **frequently wrong**. It is grounded
in the documents you supplied, which reduces invention but does not eliminate it. Do not rely on it
for anything that matters, and do not present it as fact.
